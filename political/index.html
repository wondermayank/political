The Political Ladder

How India is governed

Elections, not exams, fill this ladder. Every rank below answers one question: who has the power to make policy, and how did they get it?

The full climb

Village to Republic, rung by rung

01

Gram Panchayat

A village's own government — Sarpanch and Panchs, elected locally.

02

MLA (Vidhayak)

One elected member per assembly constituency, sitting in the state's Vidhan Sabha.

03

Chief Minister

Leader of the party/alliance with a majority in the state assembly — head of the state government.

04

MP (Lok Sabha)

One elected member per parliamentary constituency, sitting in Parliament's lower house.

05

Prime Minister

Leader of the party/alliance with a majority in the Lok Sabha — head of the union government.

06

President of India

Elected by an electoral college of all MPs and MLAs — the ceremonial head of state.
