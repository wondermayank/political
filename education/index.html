The Service Ladder

How India is served

Exams, not elections, fill this ladder. Every officer below — from an SDM to the Cabinet Secretary — reached their post by clearing a competitive exam and earning a rank.

The full climb

School to the top of the civil services

01

Board exams (10th & 12th)

The starting line — school-leaving exams that decide eligibility for higher education streams.

02

Graduation

Any bachelor's degree from a recognised university — the minimum qualification for almost every major government exam.

03

State-level exams (SSC, State PCS, Banking)

State Public Service Commissions and SSC fill state and clerical/inspector-level government posts.

04

UPSC Civil Services Exam

The national exam: three stages, deciding entry into IAS, IPS, IFS and 20+ central services.

05

Training

Selected officers train at academies like LBSNAA (IAS) or SVPNPA (IPS) before taking charge.

06

Field posting & promotion

Officers begin as SDM/ASP-rank and rise over a career toward District Magistrate, Secretary, or DGP-level posts.
