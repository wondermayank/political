The UPSC Civil Services Exam

One exam, three stages, and a single all-India rank that decides an entire career.

Conducted once a year by the Union Public Service Commission, the CSE is the gateway to the IAS, IPS, IFS (Foreign Service), and over 20 other Central Services. Roughly 5–10 lakh candidates apply each year for around 800–1000 final vacancies.

Three stages

Prelims → Mains → Interview

01

Preliminary Exam

Two objective-type papers (General Studies + CSAT). Only a screening test — marks don't count toward the final rank, only qualifying matters.

02

Main Exam

Nine descriptive papers over several days, including an optional subject, essay, and general studies — this is where most of the final rank is earned.

03

Personality Test (Interview)

A board interview assessing judgement, awareness and temperament — worth a smaller but decisive share of the final score.

04

Final Merit List

Mains + Interview marks are combined into a single all-India rank, published as the final result.

After the result

Rank decides everything next

Service preference form

Before results, candidates rank their preferred services — IAS, IPS, IFS, IRS, etc. — in order of choice.

Rank + preference + vacancy

A candidate's final rank, their stated preference order, and category-wise vacancies together decide which service they're allotted.

Cadre allocation

Selected candidates are also assigned a state cadre — the state where they'll serve for most of their career.
