Beyond IAS and IPS

Other Central Services & the Police Hierarchy

The UPSC CSE fills more than two services — and the police force has its own long climb from constable to IPS-level command.

Other UPSC-selected services

Where the rest of the merit list goes

IRS — Indian Revenue Service

Administers direct and indirect taxes — Income Tax and Customs/GST departments.

IFS — Indian Foreign Service

Diplomatic corps representing India abroad, covered in more detail on the IAS/IPS page.

IRTS / IRPS

Indian Railway services — managing traffic operations and personnel across the railway network.

IPoS — Indian Postal Service

Manages India's postal network and postal-linked financial services.

IDAS / IDES

Handles defence accounts and estate management for the armed forces.

IAAS

Indian Audit & Accounts Service — audits government spending under the CAG.

The police ladder

From Constable to Director General

01

Constable

Entry-level, recruited through state police recruitment boards — no UPSC involvement at this stage.

02

Head Constable / Assistant Sub-Inspector

Promotion-based ranks within the state police, reached through seniority and departmental exams.

03

Sub-Inspector (SI)

Often a direct-entry rank via a state-level exam; leads a police station's day-to-day operations.

04

Deputy Superintendent of Police (DSP)

Reached either by State PCS exam or promotion from SI — oversees multiple police stations in a sub-division.

05

IPS ranks — SP, IG, DGP

Entered via UPSC CSE (as covered on the IAS/IPS page) or by promotion from DSP after a long, competitive career; DGP heads the entire state police force.
